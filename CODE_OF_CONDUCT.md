# Code of Conduct

We want collaboration to remain technical, respectful, and accessible.

Participants must:

- Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, or intimidation.
- Respect contributors across languages, cultures, disabilities, experience levels, and communication styles.
- Keep reviews focused on observable behavior, standards, tests, and maintainability.
- Avoid publishing private information or security-sensitive material.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these rules, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Conduct concerns may be reported privately to **contact@healthrenewal.org** with the subject `Rawafid Toolkit conduct report`. Reports should include enough context for the maintainers to understand the incident, but reporters should avoid sending credentials, secrets, medical information, or unrelated personal data.

Do not use a public GitHub issue for a report that would expose private or sensitive information. Security vulnerabilities should follow the private reporting process in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) instead of this conduct-reporting path.

Reports will be reviewed by the project steward or an appropriate maintainer who is not directly involved in the reported incident. The project will limit disclosure to what is reasonably necessary to investigate and respond. Possible responses include clarification, moderation, removal of content, contribution restrictions, or exclusion from project spaces, depending on severity and recurrence.

If the maintainer community grows, the project will expand the reporting path so that concerns involving one maintainer can be reviewed independently by another maintainer or designated community contact.
