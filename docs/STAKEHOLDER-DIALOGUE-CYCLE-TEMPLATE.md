## 4. Discussion guide

Use the core questions in [STAKEHOLDER-DIALOGUE.md](./STAKEHOLDER-DIALOGUE.md). For the initial governance-readiness cycle, record responses or `not answered` for these required prompts:

1. Which open-source communities have you seen governed effectively, and what made their governance work in practice?
2. Which project decisions would you want to participate in, and which should remain delegated?
3. What should this project avoid as it formalizes governance?

Also record:

- evidence cited by the participant;
- uncertainty, conflict of interest, or organizational constraints they disclosed;
- corrections requested before publication;
- whether a quotation is authorized and in what form.

## 7. Publication and retention check

- [ ] The public record contains no private contact details or unnecessary sensitive data.
- [ ] Names, roles, organizations, and quotations match each participant's attribution choice.
- [ ] Silence is not represented as consent or support.
- [ ] Participation is not represented as endorsement, partnership, accreditation, sponsorship, certification, or formal affiliation.
- [ ] Coverage gaps and their likely effect are disclosed.
- [ ] Private raw notes have an owner and a justified retention/deletion date.
