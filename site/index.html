Claim boundaries

What this project deliberately does not claim

  • No automatic WCAG certification or blanket accessibility conformance.
  • No full Unicode Bidirectional Algorithm, UTS #39, or UAX #29 conformance claim.
  • No provider endorsement, academic endorsement, or partner approval unless independently evidenced.
  • No npm publication or provenance claim until a public registry release can be verified.
